所闻，即所见。Hear it. Read it.

监听电脑正在播放的声音，把外语播客、视频和会议，实时变成原文与译文的双语字幕。识别和翻译都在这台机器上完成，内置中英文，可选 29+ 种语言。 It listens to whatever your computer is playing and turns foreign podcasts, videos, and meetings into live bilingual subtitles: source text on top, translation below. Recognition and translation both run on your machine. Chinese and English are built in, with 29+ languages available.

07 · 常见问题FAQ

常见问题解答Frequently asked questions

音频会上传吗？Is my audio uploaded?

不会。识别与翻译都在本机完成，音频与文本不上传。可选模型下载与检查更新需要联网；应用内的 Aptabase 匿名统计不含音频与文本，可在「通用 → 隐私」中关闭。No. Recognition and translation run locally; audio and text are not uploaded. Optional model downloads and update checks need network. The app's anonymous Aptabase statistics contain no audio or text and can be switched off under General, Privacy.

支持哪些语言？Which languages are supported?

内置 Opus-MT 支持英语 ↔ 中文，翻译方向需要手动选择。识别一侧：X-ASR 做中英混说流式，Zipformer 做中英双语，Nemotron 3.5 覆盖 28 种语言（模型内 40 种），Qwen3-ASR 覆盖 30 种。翻译一侧：LMT-60 覆盖 29 种（Apache-2.0），NLLB-200 覆盖 30 种（仅限非商业用途）。macOS 26+ 还可以使用系统语音识别与系统翻译，目标语言取决于已安装的语言包。Opus-MT, bundled, covers English ↔ Chinese and you choose the direction. For recognition, X-ASR streams mixed Chinese and English, Zipformer handles both languages, Nemotron 3.5 covers 28 languages (40 in the model), and Qwen3-ASR covers 30. For translation, LMT-60 covers 29 languages under Apache-2.0, and NLLB-200 covers 30 but is not licensed for commercial use. On macOS 26 or later, system speech recognition and system translation are also available; the target languages depend on the language packs installed.

需要安装 BlackHole 之类的虚拟声卡吗？Do I need BlackHole or another virtual audio driver?

不需要。macOS 使用 Core Audio Process Tap，Windows 使用 WASAPI loopback。首次采集系统声音时需要授予屏幕与系统音频录制权限。No. macOS uses Core Audio Process Tap and Windows uses WASAPI loopback. The first system audio capture asks for Screen and System Audio Recording permission.

为什么开始后要等一两秒才出字？Why does the first line take a second or two?

首次开始需要加载识别与翻译模型，默认模型实测约 1.25–2.2 秒，之后进入流式输出。Qwen3 按句出字，首次加载也更久。The first run loads the recognition and translation models. With the default model it takes about 1.25 to 2.2 seconds, then streams. Qwen3 emits whole sentences and takes longer to load the first time.

能在全屏视频或游戏上显示吗？Does it work over full screen video or games?

macOS 上悬浮窗会被换类为 NSPanel，可以盖住其他应用的全屏窗口。Windows 上除独占全屏游戏外都可以。GNOME Wayland 不支持普通窗口置顶，见下一条。On macOS the overlay is retyped as an NSPanel and covers other apps in full screen. On Windows it works everywhere except exclusive full screen games. GNOME Wayland has no keep above for regular windows; see the next question.

Linux 上悬浮窗不置顶？The overlay will not stay on top on Linux?

Wayland 协议不支持普通窗口置顶，GNOME 等 Wayland 桌面下悬浮窗会被其他窗口盖住。需要置顶时用 GDK_BACKEND=x11 启动（走 XWayland）；X11 桌面默认正常。Wayland has no keep above for regular windows, so on GNOME and other Wayland desktops the overlay can be covered by other windows. Launch with GDK_BACKEND=x11 (XWayland) to pin it; X11 desktops work out of the box.

免费吗？Is it free?

免费，MIT 开源，源码在 GitHub。可选的多语言模型单独下载；NLLB-200 仅限非商业用途，其他模型均为 Apache-2.0 或同等级许可。Yes. It is free and open source under MIT, with the code on GitHub. Multilingual models are separate downloads; NLLB-200 is not licensed for commercial use, and the other models are Apache-2.0 or equivalent.

支持哪些系统？Which systems are supported?

macOS 14.2 及以上的 Apple Silicon、Windows 10 及以上、Ubuntu 24。macOS 26 及以上额外支持系统语音识别与系统翻译。当前不提供 Intel Mac 安装包。macOS 14.2 or later on Apple Silicon, Windows 10 or later, and Ubuntu 24. macOS 26 or later adds system speech recognition and system translation. There is no Intel Mac build at the moment.
